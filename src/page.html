Kits and crests for Inazuma Fantasy · version 2.0

Connect Fantasy’s «mods» folder

It is the mods folder inside the game’s folder, next to nie.exe (VRFW, BetterQoL and the other mods are in it). For example:

…\Inazuma Eleven Fantasy\app_2799860\depot_2799861\mods

Then you can also connect the reference folder (the game’s files, read only): it gives you every kit base, the game’s crests, the 3D view and the player cards. Without it you get the kits without sponsors and crests made from your own pictures.

Your browser will ask for permission to see and change files in that folder. The app reads the bases already there and only writes inside mods\CustomKits. Nothing leaves your computer.

Close the game before you generate or delete a kit or a crest.
